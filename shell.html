    The Battle of Austerlitz

    Blue is the French army. The Allies are green for Russia and white for Austria, and their movement arrows are drawn in amber. The high ground in the centre is the Pratzen plateau, and it decides the battle.

    Drag to move the map, right-drag to turn it, scroll to zoom, and click any formation to see who it was and what it was doing.
