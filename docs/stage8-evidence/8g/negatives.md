# Negatives: searched and not found (texts on the villages' buildings and landmarks)

Compiled 29 October 2026 by a machine reader. A negative is only as good as the search: each says what was searched, how, and where.
"Text layer" means the archive.org or BSB OCR, searched with regular expressions (long s and the Fraktur umlauts normalised; place
names in their period spellings: Sokolnitz, Telnitz/Tellnitz, Kobelnitz, Augezd/Aujezd/Aujest, Pratzen/Pratze/Prazen, Blasowitz/
Blaziowitz, Girzikowitz/Ghirzikowitz, Puntowitz, Schlapanitz, Krzenowitz/Kreznowitz, Kruh, Bosenitz, Posoritz/Posorzitz, Holubitz,
Satschan, Menitz/Mönitz, Bellowitz, Santon, and the Russian forms Соколниц, Тельниц, Аугест, Праце(н), Блазовиц, Кобельниц,
Пунтовиц, Сачан). "Window" searches look for the words within a stated number of characters of a place name. OCR misses words; a
text-layer negative is weaker than a page read on the image, and is labelled so. The scripts are in
`the reading session's scripts (not committed): ` (`roofwin.py`, `firewin.py`,
`kwwin.py`, `kwwin2.py`, `edgewin_ru.py`, `kw.py`, `search.py`).
