# Landmarks of the Austerlitz battlefield: modern positions in the map's frame

Research note, 2026-10-28. Read-only with respect to the repository: `geo.js` (GEOREF) and the generated world module were only required, nothing under `the repository` was changed.
Machine-readable companion: `places.json` (same folder). Raw query results: `raw/`. Working figures in the map frame: `fig_sokolnice_mapframe.svg`, `fig_highway_mapframe.svg`.

**Conventions.** `map` = `GEOREF.toMap(lat, lon)` (x right, y down, 31.647 units per km, frame turned 17.4° from true north); `world` = `[(mx-340)*0.5, (my-250)*0.5]` (in `places.json`). "Offset" = distance from the map's current point to the modern position, and the **true bearing of that move** (0° = north), from `GEOREF.kmBetween` and `GEOREF.bearingDeg` on map coordinates. Positions are the area centroid of the building's footprint in the Czech address/building register (RÚIAN, ČÚZK), which OpenStreetMap imports; every other source is listed with its distance from that centroid. "Map now" for a church is the integer village point of `VILLAGES` (world.js lines 103-112), where `buildSettlements` stands the church (the `CHURCHES.indexOf` block). These points are within 0.5 map units (about 15 m) of the `GEOREF.GT` points. World.js line numbers below are those of HEAD `51d4a15`; the working tree is being edited by other work, which has shifted some lines down by about 32 (for example the castle block is at line 1508 there), with the values unchanged.

## What stands out

1. **The Sokolnice pheasantry lies north of the castle, not south-east of the village.** The walled enclosure (zámecká bažantnice / obora; 1.20 km north-south by 0.58 km east-west, about 0.50 km² inside its outline) runs north from the castle park toward Kobylnice; its centroid is **2,298 m due north (358°) of the drawn wood's centre (236, 385)**. The drawn ellipse (1.28 km²) and the real enclosure **do not overlap at all**. In the map frame the enclosure spans x 201.4-219.9, y 297.2-338.1 (the drawn ellipse: x 212-260, y 368-402). The NPÚ heritage record dates the obora to the 2nd quarter of the 18th century, so it existed in 1805. The Goldbach (Říčka / Zlatý potok) runs through the middle of it.
2. **The Sokolnice castle is 535 m north-west of the drawn castle.** Real: 672 m from the village point at 31° (NNE); drawn: 651 m at 78° (E). The distance was right, the direction about 48° off. The real block is about 62 x 52 m; the drawn complex (world.js 1476-1487, `W(226,355)`) is about 440 m across its wings with a wall ring of about 440 x 320 m radii, drawn at the same symbolic scale as the village houses (1.4-2.4 world units, about 90-150 m). The Baroque granary stands 187 m south-west of the castle.
3. **The drawn Goldbach at Sokolnice runs 0.3-0.5 km west of the real stream.** At the castle's and the village's map rows (y 340-365) the Říčka (OSM and ZABAGED agree) is at x 215-221; the drawn Goldbach is at x 204-205. In reality the village core, the castle and the granary are **west** of the stream (castle 145 m, village point 206 m) and the pheasantry straddles it; on the map the village point is **east** of the drawn Goldbach. Moving the castle to its true place without moving the stream would leave it 255 m east of the drawn Goldbach, still on the wrong bank. (The modern channel is regulated; the 1805 bed was not checked, see below.)
4. **The Posoritz post house (Stará pošta, Kovalovice č.p. 109) is 1,367 m south-east (132°) of `GT.posthouse`**, outside its stated uncertainty of about 1 km. It stands on road II/430, the old imperial road. The drawn highway east of the Santon turns north-east through Tvarožná and runs **0.5-1.2 km north of the real road** (465 m at x 240, 1,062 m at x 324, 1,232 m at x 363); west of the Santon it is within 120-260 m (gaps measured along map y). Placed at its true position the post house would be 1,070 m off the drawn highway, so the highway east of the Santon needs the same correction. Three other candidates are rejected: the inn across the road (No. 108); the building in Pozořice village, which was the post only from 1879; and Rousínov's old post, which is the next station (Rausnitz).
5. **Five of the twelve drawn churches are not churches of 1805.** Blažovice's church was built in 1930-1934 (the belfry on the green dates from 1831-1832; the manor house of c. 1725 stands at the village point). Holubice's church dates from 1929, although the chapel of St Gotthard of 1769 stands 18 m from the village point. Jiříkovice has only a small belfry (whether it predates 1805 is disputed) and a Baroque manor farm. Kobylnice has only a belfry ("beginning of the 19th century"). At Sokolnice the chapel at the village point is given as 1886 by RÚIAN; the 1805 sacral building was the castle chapel. Two more are later buildings: Tvarožná (1880-81, on the medieval church's site) and Újezd (1845-47; whether it stands on the old church's site is not established).
6. **Prace church is the 1728-1730 building** (its tower was replaced with a lower one after 1805), and it stands **399 m south** of the Pratzen village point, on the rise at the south end of the village.
7. **The Slavkov château garden is west of the château; the drawn "Austerlitz park" lies 1.6 km south-east of the town.** The real garden's centroid is 1,990 m north-west of the drawn wood's centre (551, 151); the two do not touch.
8. The approximations that were checked hold: `GT.chapel` (St Anthony) is within 107 m of the chapel, and `GT.santon` within 20 m of the Santon chapel.
9. **Village street directions.** The map's lane direction (world.js `laneDir`) differs from the old core's axis by 60-88° in 10 of the 16 villages (Table B); it is close only at Sokolnice (1°), Kobylnice (17°) and Měnín (19°).

## Notes by place

### Sokolnice: castle, granary, pheasantry
- **Castle** (zámek Sokolnice, Zámecká 57): RÚIAN building 13158571 = OSM relation/1387462; ZABAGED draws the same block as a "zámek". NPÚ 1000120787: "a historicist château made from a Baroque building on the site of an older fort … with an extensive park enclosed by a wall … an early Baroque chapel". cs.wikipedia gives this history: a Renaissance tvrz of 1560; bought by the Dietrichsteins in 1705 and rebuilt as a two-storey Baroque château with a chapel (architect F. A. Grimm); bought by the Mitrovský family in 1843 and made neo-Gothic in the mid-19th century. The battlefield list says it burned during the battle and calls the rebuild after 1848 neo-Renaissance. The two style labels disagree; the site does not change. The castle chapel of the Exaltation of the Holy Cross is OSM node/3384317516, inside the block.
- **Granary** (sýpka, RÚIAN 46430121 = OSM way/120172453; NPÚ 1000153004, Wikidata Q38134887): about 506 m², OSM `building:levels` 4 with a half-hipped roof. NPÚ: "monumental Baroque utility building from the first quarter of the 18th century". In 1805 it was a French strongpoint and afterwards a prison for Allied prisoners (cs.wikipedia; 400 Russians according to the village article). It has been flats since 2020. Not drawn on the map; it lies 499 m from the village point at 24°.
- **Pheasantry**: no OSM feature is named "bažantnice". The enclosure is identified by:
  - the gate "vstup do bažantnice" (OSM node/4810832354) at the castle park's north edge;
  - the streets `U Bažantnice` and `Bažantnice`;
  - the NPÚ records "obora" (1000120787_0004, at 49.122991, 16.729194) and "ohradní zeď obory" (_0005: a brick wall with five marked French gun positions; the battlefield list says the marking is secondary and doubted);
  - ZABAGED's forest polygons and its 925 m wall along the east side (OSM barrier=wall way/488717833, 733 m, and others).

  The outline used is the outer ring of OSM relation/7163945 (landuse=forest, 0.455 km² of woodland after its clearings, about 0.50 km² inside the outline). Its map-frame outline, the walls and the gate are in `places.json` → `checks.pheasantry`. The real centroid is 884 m from the real castle at 16°; the drawn wood lies 999 m from the drawn castle at 179°. **The direction is reversed.**
- **Not verified:** the enclosure's extent in 1805 and the 1805 bed of the Goldbach at Sokolnice. ### Prace, Telnice, Újezd
- **Prace church** (RÚIAN 46052721, NPÚ 1000134522, Wikidata Q12030519, ÚSKP 23515/7-908). cs.wikipedia's history: a Romanesque church of about 1274, destroyed in the Thirty Years' War; **on its site the present Baroque church of 1728-1730**. Its tower, a Russian observation post during the battle, was hit by French guns and later replaced with a lower one (1810 according to the battlefield list). A chapel and sacristy were added in 1884, and the tower was made slimmer and taller in the early 1930s. The NPÚ annotation read here says only "Baroque sacral architecture rebuilt in a Classicist manner"; the catalogue page itself (pamatkovykatalog.cz/x-14886558) returned HTTP 503. Wikidata gives inception "18th century".
- **Telnice church** (RÚIAN 50266829, NPÚ 1000148882): Baroque, 1726-1734, on the site of the Romanesque-Gothic church whose tower survives. In 1805 the French defence of Telnice centred on it, and Napoleon slept at the rectory on 27 November (cs.wikipedia).
- **Újezd SS Peter and Paul** (RÚIAN 39874931, NPÚ 1000130835): "a building of 1845-1846" (NPÚ); 1845-1847, consecrated 1852, replacing a Romanesque church first recorded in 1318 (cs.wikipedia). Whether it stands on the old church's site is not stated.
- **St Anthony of Padua chapel** (RÚIAN 39874940, NPÚ 1000141624): "a building of 1862 standing where an older chapel stood, where the first shots of the battle of Austerlitz fell" (NPÚ). The 1703 chapel, closed in 1785, burned; the present chapel was built "on the same place" in 1863 (battlefield list). `GT.chapel` is 107 m from it; the move would be 205° (SSW).

### Santon chapel
RÚIAN 46085165, NPÚ 1000151014: "a building of 1832 put up where the original wooden chapel of 1700 stood". cs.wikipedia ("Santon (kopec)") says the chapel, deconsecrated in 1776, was pulled down by the French when they fortified the hill. The chapel is 20 m from `GT.santon` (the PeakVisor summit).

### The Posoritz post house and the highway
- **Stará pošta / Stará pozořická pošta, Kovalovice č.p. 109.** The RÚIAN address point "č.p. 109, 68301 Kovalovice" (kód 23956321) lies in building 23571594 (306 m²). Wikidata Q141367424 ("Stará pozořická pošta", address "Kovalovice 109") and the cs.wikipedia article "Stará Pošta (Kovalovice)" (coordinate 49.1851, 16.8261) agree within 25 m.
- Its history, from that article: an inn on the imperial road; a hereditary post station of the Liechtenstein Pozořice estate set up in house No. 109 in 1785; named "Pozořická pošta" on the 1826 map of Kovalovice; the post moved to Pozořice village (No. 60) in 1879; Murat's headquarters on 28 November, Bagration's on 1-2 December, Napoleon's after the battle. The main building stands parallel to the road (II/430). It is not a listed monument in the NPÚ layers read.
- **Rejected candidates:**
  - Kovalovice No. 108, across the road: an inn (the manorial 'Kadlecův dům'), not the post (same article); RÚIAN address point 49.184772, 16.827377.
  - Pozořice village, "správní budova - býv. pošta" (NPÚ record 138729_1, 49.208735, 16.792271; the nearest RÚIAN buildings there are Nos. 1 and 4, not the No. 60 the article names): the post moved into Pozořice only in 1879, so no building there was the post house of 1805. It lies 2.2 km north-west of `GT.posthouse`, off the highway.
  - Rousínov's Stará pošta on the town square (NPÚ 1000121971, mid-18th century): the Rausnitz station, 5.3 km east.
- **The old road:** OSM ways with `ref=430` from Bedřichovice to Rousínov. In map coordinates the real road's y is 104.4 at the Santon's x = 222 (the Santon is north of the road, as data.js says), 94.2 at x 240, 75.7 at x 300, 69.6 at x 324 and 54.7 at x 363 (Stará pošta). The drawn highway's y is 98.1, 79.5, 48.4, 36.0 and 15.7 at the same x. ### The other villages' churches

- **Šlapanice:** church of the Assumption (NPÚ 1000140666): medieval, made Baroque after the mid-18th century; Allied prisoners were held in it after the battle. Extra: the château (now a grammar school; RÚIAN 23691972) was a French field hospital.
- **Jiříkovice:**
  - Belfry on the green (RÚIAN 43615511, 8 m²; NPÚ 1000124062: "19th century"). The battlefield list says the belfry and the manor farm were the only buildings to survive the burning of the village, which would place the belfry before 1805; the two sources disagree.
  - Manor farm (NPÚ 1000119615): "a Baroque farm probably on the site of a late-medieval fort; a military hospital in the 1805 campaign".
  - There is no church.
- **Blažovice:** the church of the Sacred Heart, built 1930-1934 (cs.wikipedia); the belfry-chapel on the green of 1831-1832 (battlefield list); the **former "castle"**, Wikidata Q122924573 "zámeček", the former seat of the manorial officials, Náves No. 36, RÚIAN building 23713933. hrady.cz (citing F. ## Method and certainty
- **Positions.** The primary position is the area centroid of the RÚIAN building polygon (cadastral survey; OSM's footprints are imported from it). Where no building applies, it is the OSM polygon centroid (pheasantry, Slavkov park) or the NPÚ point (Pozořice, Rousínov, the Jiříkovice manor). For single buildings the alternatives (OSM, the NPÚ definition point, Wikidata, the cs.wikipedia list) agree within 1-31 m; the one exception is the list's St Anthony point, 82 m off on the hilltop path. The positional uncertainty of a building is a few metres, below the map's resolution (one map unit = 31.6 m).
- **Identity and date.** These rest on the NPÚ annotations (heritage catalogue text, read through the geoportal), cs.wikipedia articles and lists (the revision ids are in `places.json`) and, for Blažovice, hrady.cz. None of these is a primary source for 1805. Each 1805 status is labelled as the sources give it, and conflicts are kept: the Jiříkovice belfry's date, and the style of the Sokolnice castle's rebuild.
- **Streets.** These are today's streets. Their axis is a reasonable proxy for the old core but not a survey of 1805.
- **Not reached:**
  - Overpass API (all mirrors: connection reset or timeout through the egress proxy), so the OSM API 0.6 `map` call was used instead;
  - pamatkovykatalog.cz (HTTP 503), so its data was read from the NPÚ geoportal's ArcGIS layers;
  - www.austerlitz.org (connection reset);
  - the Wikimedia APIs were rate-limited at times (HTTP 429); the needed pages were fetched once they answered.
