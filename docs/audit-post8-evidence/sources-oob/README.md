# Audit after Stage 8: sources-oob (the source-verification ledger for the forces)

Build audited: `austerlitz-command-map.html`, md5 `f10fe585c46aea576f436ed51ac46f83`, origin/main `43e3e5b`. Done on 2 November 2026 by a
machine reader (an audit agent); nothing here was read by a person. No application source, data, test, threshold, archive or build was
changed; no repository check was run (they were running in the background).

## What was examined

The most consequential claims about the forces, as a visitor meets them: the two army totals (the headquarters' compact card, the first
card a selection opens, `app.js:7811`; `data.js:152`, `:444`), every corps, column and division strength (the compact card's "Strength" row,
`app.js:7812-7813`, which shows no note and no tag; the full dossier's "How many" section, `app.js:7974-7978`) with its note,
the figures quoted in the themes (`analysis.js:25`, `:40`), the method statements on strengths (`SOURCE_NOTE.body[2]`, `.body[5]`,
`.refs[0]`), the decisions named in the brief (184 Kienmayer 6,340; 202 (b) Bourcier with Friant; 210 (c) the Guard infantry's commander;
213 (b) the Russian Guard after Duffy pp. 184-185), a sample of commanders and compositions, the most visible dress claims (French line
infantry, Russian musketeers, Austrian line infantry, the Szekler hussars of decisions 195 and 200) and four of `ORDNANCE`'s eleven claims
(8E), with the drivers on the guns on the heights.

For each claim: where it is shown, the label a visitor sees (the dossier's claim tag is `CLAIM_WORDS[cl].w`, `app.js:8117`; the
headline strength and guns rows carry none), the cited source and the register entry, then the cited material itself where it could be
reached. 42 ledger entries: `ledger.md` (table and evidence) and `ledger.json` (the same, structured), generated by
`tools/audit-post8/sources-oob/make-ledger.py`.

## How (and what was run)

- **Page images** (archive.org, IIIF, e-rara), downloaded with `curl` into the session's scratchpad and read on the image, zoomed with
  PIL crops: Schönhals 1873 pp. 177-178 (the Allied order of battle; `derkriegindeuts00schgoog` n181-n182); Sokolov 2006 t. II pp. 183-200
  (`1799-1805-.-ii` n182-n199: the French returns of 22 November with the presumed 2 December figures, and the Allied order of battle);
  Mikhailovsky-Danilevsky 1844 p. 178 (IIIF `1805-.-bmk-brz$189`); Correspondance t. XI p. 450 (the 30th Bulletin; n459); Gassendi 1809 t. I
  pp. 110 and 440; Alombert and Colin t. I p. 228 and t. IV p. 183; Fieffé 1854 t. II p. 17 (e-rara canvas 26006711); Viskovatov t. 10
  p. 196. Crops of the public-domain pages are in `pages/` (no Sokolov or Duffy images are kept: works in copyright).
- **Text layers**: archive.org's full-text search inside Duffy 1977 (`austerlitz18050000duff`, a lending item whose pages cannot be opened
  here), by `python3 -I tools/audit-post8/sources-oob/ia-fts.py austerlitz18050000duff <queries>`, output kept in `duffy-fts.txt` (OCR
  snippets: weaker than a page image by the project's own standard; every finding resting on them says so); the `_djvu.txt` of the
  Correspondance t. XI and of Stutterheim 1806 (`11344011bsb`), searched with `grep`.
- **Web**: one search for the origin of the "7,750 to 14,200" range; WarHistory part I fetched (gives no strengths); military-review.com
  answered 503.
- **Counts**: `node -e` over `data.js` and `appearance.js` (40 formations, 37 numeric strengths, 31 strength notes, 40 commanders, 14
  compositions, 8 gun counts, 2 army totals; 49 dress classes with 174 sourced claims; 11 `ORDNANCE` claims).
- **Arithmetic** (derived, shown in the ledger): Schönhals's and Sokolov's sums, the Austrian share, the Guard's rows, Duffy's IV Column rows.

## What was found (details and refs in `ledger.md`; the findings are in the structured result)

1. **Figures attributed to Duffy that Duffy does not print** (text layer): the III Corps detachment's 4,300 with 830 cavalry and Friant's
   3,470 (Duffy: Friant 3,800 and 9 guns, Bourcier 2,500 and 3 guns), also in the "weakness" theme and `COMPOSITION.friant`; the IV Column's
   13,900 (Duffy: 23,900, the figure the note gives to Chandler, with the same 52 light and 24 heavy pieces; his rows add to about 16,000)
   and the Russian wing's 4,800 "implied" from it; Kienmayer's "3,440 foot and 3,440 horse, which make 6,880" (Duffy: 6,780; 3,440 is
   Sokolov's infantry); Dokhturov's 13,240 infantry (Duffy: 13,650). The guns agree every time.
2. **Two key formations drawn below every readable figure, the notes omitting them**: the III Corps detachment (Sokolov pp. 188-189:
   presumed 3,800 + 1,500 = 5,300, a total the register already carries from p. 193; Bourcier's 830 has no source) and the IV Column
   (Schönhals 16,118, Sokolov 15,900 + 300; the Russian wing 6,965 in Schönhals against 4,800 drawn).
3. **Bare working figures above the 22 November returns**: the French Guard cavalry 2,500 (Sokolov: 1,023 horse), Kellermann 2,400,
   d'Hautpoul 1,300, Walther 1,500, Rivaud 6,000, Drouet 7,000, with no note; and no claim tag on any headline strength or gun count
   (the compact card, the first view of a selected formation, shows the strength with neither note nor tag for every formation).
4. **"Kologrivov's command on 2 December is in Sokolov alone"** is contradicted by Schönhals p. 178 ("GL. Kollowrizow" over the Guards),
   which the project's own stage 6B reading noted as "a second source for that name", and by Duffy's heading; step 4's negative search used
   patterns that cannot match that spelling. The Russian Guard's rows that decision 213 (b) took from the owner's photograph of Duffy were
   already printed in Schönhals's table, read since step 4.
5. **"Dokhturov's I Column is given as anything from 7,750 to 14,200 men"** among "reputable orders of battle": the sources read give
   12,730-14,000; 7,752 appears on a popular website.
6. Smaller: the army totals' notes omit figures the project read (Mikhailovsky-Danilevsky's 90,000, Duffy's 60,000 effective; Duffy's
   80,000-87,000 for the 85,400 credited to him); locator slips (Fieffé's "footnote" is body text; the musketeer coat cites the grenadier
   table); stronger sources uncredited (the 30th Bulletin for Bourcier with Friant; Duffy for figures the map calls its own).
7. **Verified** on page images: IV Corps 23,600 and its divisions' 22 November figures; Kienmayer 6,340 and Sokolov's 6,380; the V Column's
   5,600 / 4,600 / 3,600; the Russian Guard's 4,430 and 2,900 (independently of Duffy, through Schönhals); the Guard infantry's commander not
   established; the 30th Bulletin's forty and twenty guns; Bourcier up from Raigern with Friant; the French line infantry's coat; the
   Gribeauval system, wheels, team and IV Corps' letter on drivers (an inference, labelled as one).

## What could not be checked, and why

- **Duffy 1977's pages**: a lending item; only its OCR text layer could be searched. A person should read pp. 179-185 on the page (as the
  owner did for pp. 184-185) before any data change.
- **Smith 1998**: not readable (as register S5.8 found). Some figures attributed jointly to "Duffy and Smith" may be Smith's alone.
- **The 1805 Schematismus** (the Szekler hussars, grade A): Hungaricana and its CDN answered 403; a retry with a browser user agent was
  refused by the session's permission classifier and not pursued; no other scan was found.
- **Teuber 1895** (the Austrian white coat): the Innsbruck digital library timed out.
- Chandler 1966, Goetz, Castle, Bowden: in copyright, not tried. WarHistory's "about 12,000" (part I gives no strengths; part II not
  fetched); Napoleon-Empire's 17,000; the Tvarožná municipal history's 1,600 at the Santon: not fetched.
- Not covered for want of time: 34 of the 40 commanders, 10 of the 14 compositions, 170 of the 174 dress claims, 7 of the 11 `ORDNANCE`
  claims, the losses, and the sensitivity of the derived readings (the holding on the heights; tour stop 4's 39,000 and 19,000) to the
  strengths questioned here.
