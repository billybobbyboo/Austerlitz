## Claims

### How to read the Schematismus facing line (applies to every `schem1805` claim)

Each regiment's entry in `schem1805` closes with a boxed line giving its distinguishing colour and its buttons, e.g.
"Pompadour mit Nro. 18. | g. Kn." ("pompadour [red], with Nr. 18 | yellow buttons"). "g. Kn." = gelbe Knöpfe (yellow,
i.e. brass), "w. Kn." = weiße Knöpfe (white, i.e. tin/pewter): the abbreviations are not expanded in the volume (the
list of abbreviations on p. 5-6 does not include them), so the expansion is an **inference** from usage. "mit Nro. N"
names the regiment that shares the same colour (the pair is reciprocal: IR 1 reads "Pompadour mit Nro. 18 | g. Kn.",
IR 18 reads "Pompadour mit Nro. 1 | w. Kn."; the buttons tell the pair apart): an **inference** from the reciprocity, which
holds for every pair checked on the page images: IR 1/18, 8/23, 9/54, 14/58, 20/35, 24/46, 29/55, 30/49, Grenz 7/8 and 14/15. The volume does not
state that this colour is the "Egalisierung" (facing): for infantry and cuirassiers it gives only the one colour; for hussars
and uhlans it describes the whole uniform. That the single colour is the facing colour is an **inference** (the volume never
names coat colours for infantry, cuirassiers or chevaulegers). It is supported by the corps whose coat was not white: there
the line names two colours, e.g. the artillery "Rehfarbe und Ponceau. | g. Kn." and the Generalquartiermeister-Stab
"Dunkelgrün und Schwarz. | g. Kn." (claims at.art.schem1805 and at.staff.gqms.schem1805), i.e. coat and facing.
