### S8.17 `corr11_1863.p447-448.tourner-prendre`

- **source**: `corr11_1863`, no. 9541 (the 30th Bulletin, Austerlitz, 12 frimaire an XIV, 3 December 1805), pp. 447-448 (leaves n456-n457); images: https://archive.org/download/correspondancede11napouoft/page/n456_w1600.jpg, n457_w1600.jpg, kept as `docs/wp15-evidence/pages/corr11_1863-p447-n456.jpg`, `corr11_1863-p448-n457.jpg`.
- **quote** (read on: page image): (p. 447) «Toutes ces différentes démarches remplirent leur effet. Les jeunes têtes qui dirigent les affaires russes se livrèrent sans mesure à leur présomption naturelle. Il n'était plus question de battre l'armeé fran- (p. 448) çaise, mais de la tourner et de la prendre : elle n'avait tant fait que par la lâcheté des Autrichiens.»
- **translation**: "All these various steps had their effect. The young heads who run Russian affairs gave themselves up without measure to their natural presumption. It was no longer a question of beating the French army, but of turning it and taking it: it had done so much only through the cowardice of the Austrians."
- **grade**: A (the Bulletin, dated 3 December 1805; on the Allied council's intentions it reports at second hand). **label**: fact.
- **settles**: The 30th Bulletin's account of the Allied intention: to turn the French army and take it; with p. 448's 'Il n'avait qu'une crainte, c'était que l'armée française ne lui échappât' (C4.c2), an enemy expected to slip away.
- **does not settle**: what the Allied council itself wrote (the disposition read names the attack on the French right, S8.6); the road to Vienna (Mikhailovsky-Danilevsky's, S8.8).
